## Overall Impression

A disciplined, genuinely well-built dark editorial single-pager whose strongest form-level idea — the sticky-stack work chapters — is undermined by the one thing that matters most: the work is not openable. The single-accent system and reduced-motion/no-JS faithfulness prove the "engineer" half of the persona's pitch; making the work itself demonstrate the "designer" half is the single biggest opportunity.

## What's Working

1. **The single-accent discipline, held absolutely.** One signal color in focus rings, button hovers, marquee dots, disclosure hovers, the progress bar, and the contact inversion. The page demonstrates the design-system rigor it sells.
2. **The sticky-stack work chapters (desktop).** Pinning + scrubbing only the outgoing card (scale → .93, opacity → .5) makes four placeholder entries feel like a body of work. The strongest idea on the page, and carefully engineered.
3. **Reduced-motion and no-JS faithfulness.** Every animation guarded; nav and disclosures degrade gracefully. For a "I ship it so intent doesn't get lost" persona, the interaction layer proves the claim.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] The project cards are dead affordances — the portfolio's core task ends at a closed door.**
Why: The #1 action is "open and evaluate the work." Hover lifts the grayscale and scales images (1.035), which actively promises a destination; clicking yields nothing — titles aren't links, no case-study path exists. Visitors conclude the site is broken, not that case studies are coming.
Fix: Wrap title + image in real `<a href>` destinations, or, if staying single-page, replace the zoom-on-hover with a restrained cue so the card stops lying.
Suggested command: `$impeccable harden`

**[P2] The `.project__role` line carries all project metadata yet is the least visible element.**
Why: One 12px uppercase mono line holds year + discipline + engineering scope; there is no outcome/scope tier. With no openable case study, the cards are the entire evidence base, under-served by hierarchy.
Fix: Give the card system an explicit metadata grammar — year in its own slot, role/scope as a separate tier, and a result line reserved for one measurable claim — so cards skim vertically.
Suggested command: `$impeccable layout`

**[P2] Two authoritative capability lists disagree, and nav labels don't match destination headings.**
Why: `.strip` says Front-end *engineering* + Accessibility + Prototyping; `#services` says *Product design* + Front-end *development* + Design-to-code handoff. Coverage doesn't reconcile. Nav "Services" → heading "Capabilities"; "Work" → "Selected work". A persona selling design-system precision gets judged on exactly this.
Fix: One capability vocabulary and one label set; if lists stay tiered, state the tiering ("What I do / How I do it") so divergence reads as hierarchy. Align nav labels with section headings.
Suggested command: `$impeccable clarify`

**[P2] Imagery is ambient fill, not proof — and the hero gets the least specific slot on the page.**
Why: Hero media is generic "hands coding on a laptop"; all project and studio plates share one grayscale/brightness treatment, flattening every proof surface into the same mood.
Fix: Reserve hero media for the strongest real artifact (UI close-up, system diagram, before/after); differentiate plate treatment per project so four projects read as four artifacts rather than one filter applied four times.
Suggested command: `$impeccable shape`

**[P3] The conversion loop terminates in placeholders with no exit story.**
Why: `mailto:hello@example.com`, bare platform homepages (github.com/linkedin.com/read.cv), unlinked project titles, and "Selected work" with no overflow path. "I reply to everything" is promised to a fake address.
Fix: When real content lands, add a browse-more outlet inside `#work` (archive line or in-context link). Until then, label live affordances "demo" so dead ends are honest.
Suggested command: `$impeccable polish`

## Minor Observations

- During the scrub, the outgoing card at .5 opacity slides its title/role/summary beneath the next card's copy — two titles in one reading zone. Fade the outgoing *body* harder than its media so it reads artifact-over-artifact, not text-over-text.
- Hero copy restates the duality twice; the lede's second sentence is the more useful fact.
- Marquee pauses on hover but not on focus — a keyboard user can't pause it (reduced-motion wrap handles the preference case only).
- `.project__role`'s three `&middot;` separators are the typographic outlier on a page that avoids decorative punctuation.
- "Capabilities" appears twice (sr-only strip label and services h2) naming two different containers for the same concept.
- The four plate ratios (4/3, 16/10, 3/2, 5/4) share one filter, reading as sameness at different crops.

## Questions to Consider

1. The sticky-stack is depth as theater — what if the depth was the work itself (each chapter scrubbing through 2–3 frames of the actual interface: a state change, a token flip, a before/after)?
2. "Selected work" implies a selection — one line ("4 of 12 shipped, 2025") would convert the silence after project 4 into a credibility statement.
3. The page promises "no handoff" — what could a visitor *do with* the page (open one type scale, toggle one field tint) that proves the design-run-by-engineer claim in one interaction?
